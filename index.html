Quick start

Your first good cup, in five steps

  1. Pick fresh beans. Whole beans roasted within the last month, ground just before you brew.
  2. Measure the ratio. Start with 1 part coffee to 16 parts water (e.g. 15 g coffee, 240 ml water).
  3. Grind for your method. Coarser for French press, medium for pour over, finer for moka pot.
  4. Use good water. Filtered or soft tap water at around 93 °C (200 °F), just off the boil.
  5. Taste and adjust. Sour means under-extracted — grind finer or brew longer. Bitter means over-extracted — grind coarser or brew shorter.
