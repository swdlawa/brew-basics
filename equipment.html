Choosing equipment

You don't need much to brew well. A scale and a grinder matter more than an expensive machine.

The essentials

Buy these in roughly this order. Everything else is optional.

  1. A scale (0.1 g precision) — repeatability is the fastest way to improve.
  2. A grinder — a burr grinder (hand or electric) beats blade grinders and pre-ground coffee.
  3. A brewer — a French press or dripper is a cheap, capable place to start.
  4. A kettle — any kettle works; a gooseneck spout makes pour over easier.
Spend on the grinder first The single biggest upgrade for most people is freshly ground coffee from a decent burr grinder — not a fancier machine.
